AI Acquisition OS Research Desk

This publication focuses on practical AI and business automation for solopreneurs and small agencies. The goal is useful operator knowledge, not search-engine-first content.

How articles are produced

Research may use AI-assisted search, outlining, drafting and quality checks. Articles are reviewed against source evidence and edited for clarity. We distinguish documented system behavior from hypotheses, benchmarks and future plans.

Evidence standard

Time-sensitive claims should cite authoritative sources. Internal system claims are described as system evidence and are not presented as customer outcomes. No synthetic traffic, customers, revenue or testimonials are used as proof.

Commercial transparency

Some articles may eventually contain links to our own digital products or approved affiliate partners. Commercial relationships are disclosed on the relevant page. Affiliate links are secondary to the educational purpose of the publication.
