Editorial Policy

People first

Articles are created to answer real questions and help readers make decisions. Search visibility is a distribution objective, not the editorial purpose.

Originality

We prefer original explanations, worked examples, implementation patterns and transparent limitations. We do not intentionally mass-produce thin rewrites of other websites.

Sources

Time-sensitive product, platform and policy claims are checked against current first-party documentation where possible. Technical examples are labeled as examples rather than customer results.

AI assistance

AI may assist with research, organization and editing. The publication pipeline does not treat generated text as automatic evidence of expertise or accuracy.

Money

The blog uses a layered model: useful content first, then relevant digital products or approved affiliate recommendations. Affiliate relationships are disclosed. Advertising is a later-stage option and is not assumed to be the primary near-term revenue source.

Corrections

When a factual error is identified, the page should be corrected and its modified date updated. Unsupported claims are removed rather than left in place to preserve appearance.
