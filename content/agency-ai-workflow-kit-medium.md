# Agency AI Workflow Kit: A Repeatable Operating Layer for Agency Onboarding

Agencies often have the tools they need but still lose time stitching together onboarding, workflow routing, handoffs, and recurring checks.

The Agency AI Workflow Kit is a practical starting structure for teams using n8n. It focuses on reusable process structure: client intake, workflow organization, operating checklists, delivery handoffs, and repeatable execution.

The goal is not another dashboard. It is a clearer operating layer that can be adapted to an existing agency process instead of replacing everything.
