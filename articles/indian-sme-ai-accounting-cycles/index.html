How Indian SMEs Are Slashing Accounting Cycles With AI Micro-Automations

Across Tier-1 and Tier-2 industrial clusters in India, accounting departments face recurring friction with GST compliance, reconciliations, and vendor ledger verification. Rather than purchasing multi-crore ERP overhauls, progressive manufacturing and distribution SMEs are deploying deterministic local micro-automations. These lightweight local worker daemons ingest scanned PDF invoices via OCR, match line-item values against bank statements, and flag discrepancies before filing deadlines.

Early adoption metrics indicate a 65% reduction in reconciliation lead times and significant elimination of compliance penalties.
